# Compartment artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool using the user's second screenshot as a reference for composition and atmosphere. The reference's branding and UI were not included in the generated asset.

Final website asset: `assets/heritage-compartment.jpg` (421 KB). The original generated PNG is retained in the ignored `output/heritage-compartment-source.png` folder. JPEG conversion preserves the original composition. All website text, controls, ticket, animated scenery and product interactions are implemented separately in code.

## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full-screen website compartment BACKGROUND ART only, landscape 16:9. Reference image role: composition, warm painted atmosphere, seats at edges, central window. Create an original scene inside a nostalgic Indian heritage train compartment: warm tobacco-brown wood and ochre painted wall, aged bronze fittings, plush deep olive leather/vinyl opposing benches cropped at left and right foreground, curved upholstered headrests. One SINGLE large horizontal rounded-rectangle train window centered exactly at x50%, y48%; outer frame spans x29% to71%, y27% to69%. Inside of the opening should be a flat softly luminous muted pale sage surface, with NO landscape, NO bars, NO curtain: this area is a placeholder for interactive scenery composited by code. Keep the whole window straight-on, symmetrical and unobstructed. Window has thin rounded burnished brass trim, almost rectangular with modest corner radius, no interior crossbars. A small well-travelled tan leather suitcase resting on the lower left bench, a folded cream-and-rust cotton scarf and steel tiffin on it, a small clay chai cup tucked beside it; incidental human traces. Hand-painted editorial gouache/oil illustration, subtle directional brushstrokes, rich tactile uneven paint, realistic atmospheric perspective, golden late-afternoon window light, dark shadow at edges, filmic warm nostalgic mood. Upper 25% mostly warm wall with breathing space. Bottom center unobstructed dark warm wall and vent. NO people, NO text, NO letters, NO logos, NO UI, NO ticket panel, NO buttons, NO watermarks, NO app screenshot decorations. Match the rich authored luxury travel illustration atmosphere of reference, not a smooth plastic 3D render. Entire image is the train room, not a framed picture.
