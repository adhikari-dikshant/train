# Scenic collage additions

Four new assets generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then converted to WebP in `assets/` while preserving foreground transparency. The two landscapes fill the entire view behind the existing animated cutouts. The two foreground strips use the existing final entrance timing. Route, travel, scrolling and entrance easing are unchanged. Landscapes are artistic cultural composites, not documentary city views.

## Prompts and final paths

### assets/rajasthan-landscape.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: panoramic landscape background, 3:2 landscape orientation. A richly layered full-bleed panoramic landscape collage of Rajasthan, India, designed as the background behind separately animated palace, camel, peacock and musician cutouts. A warm hazy rose-gold Jaipur skyline of small havelis, intricately carved arched pavilions, old city balconies and distant fort ramparts occupies the middle third across the entire width; distant low Aravalli hills behind. An aged pale turquoise paper sky with warm ochre sun occupies upper quarter, not more. A terraced courtyard, sandstone paths, garden trees, bougainvillea, painted elephant motifs on small textile scraps, terracotta and indigo patterned torn paper layers fill the lower half and corners. A couple of small puppet and patterned pottery details within the layered foreground. Very detailed dense composition with coherent depth, a horizon and continuous scenery, not four isolated giant objects. NO large people, NO large animal, NO Hawa Mahal facade: those will be separately placed on top. NO empty beige areas. Fine torn-paper borders between layers, warm weathered material realism. Composition spans every edge with most elaborate detail upper and right edges. No words, no logos, no UI. Style: match HASSTRA's warm realistic vintage Indian travel scrapbook collages, richly textured paper and tactile gouache with detailed photographic realism softened into hand painting. Warm sandstone, rose, ochre, leafy green and muted indigo, nostalgic afternoon light. Detailed connected scenery, no cartoon, no smooth vector, no lettering or watermarks.
