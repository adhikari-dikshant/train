# This is Denim — The Cultural Express

A responsive, static campaign mockup based on the Wispr Flow meeting “Denim Cultural Promotion Campaign” (16 October 2026).

## Demonstration

Start with the carriage window. “Start your journey” opens the current destination and its garment study. “The route” opens a schematic route selector. Explore Rajasthan, Himachal Pradesh, and Kerala; use the three story tabs and numbered garment details. Each stop has a direct URL, for example `?station=kerala#piece`.

Run `python3 -m http.server 4173 --bind 127.0.0.1` and open http://127.0.0.1:4173.

## Scope and assets

This is a presentation mockup, not the complete campaign site. The Rajasthan collage was already supplied in assets. Mountain/backwater illustrations and all garment studies are original SVG concept visuals. Garment shapes and detail placement are illustrative, clearly labeled in the experience, and must be replaced with approved campaign imagery. The Kerala white denim and Himachal jacket/flag/bell directions are grounded in the meeting. No unverified historical-origin claims are included.

The route has three sample stops. CMS integration, auction/purchase functionality, complete state collection, launch dates, and final branding are outside this mockup. No visitor data is collected. Google Fonts enhances typography; local serif and sans-serif fallbacks work offline.
